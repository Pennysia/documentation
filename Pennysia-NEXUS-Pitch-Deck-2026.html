Problem · Why now

The gap

Liquidity is fragmented. Best execution is not default.

No single DEX, aggregator, RFQ, or intent venue wins across every trade size, pair, and moment. Traders settle for one app — or waste time comparing many. New venues still rebuild distribution from scratch.

Inconsistent winners

Rankings flip by trade size. A venue that wins at 100 ETH can lose at 1,000 ETH on the same pair.

High comparison cost

Checking multiple frontends burns time, gas awareness, and attention — especially when markets move.

Why now

Intents and RFQs multiplied the quote surface. Headline price ≠ executable outcome after gas and failure risk. Execution quality needs a marketplace layer.

Business model

How we earn

Aligned with better execution — not worse spreads.

Surplus fee

When a SYNC fill beats the quoted rate, Pennysia keeps the improvement, capped at 10% of output. No surplus fee if execution matches or is worse.

Intent partner fee

Hard-intent routes (CoW, UniswapX, Velora Delta) take a partner fee (~50 bps) to the Settlement fee recipient.

Protocol fee

Flat $0.50 USD charged as native ETH on Settlement swaps (converted at live ETH/USD). Exchange fees stay with the winning venue.

What we do not charge

  • No trader subscription
  • No exchange listing fee — integration is free
  • No hidden post-sign fees; costs shown before confirm

Why the model fits

  • Revenue grows with successful competitive execution
  • Exchanges keep their fee economics when they win
  • Marketplace scales by adding venues, not listing rent
